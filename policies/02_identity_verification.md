# Identity verification and who may ask

A privacy request is about one person's data, so we act only for that person.

## Verify before anything else

Before reading, exporting, correcting or deleting anything, verify the requester with **all three**:
1. the email address **registered** on the account,
2. the **last 4 digits** of their national ID,
3. the **mobile number** registered on the account.

If any of the three does not match:
- do **not** say whether an account exists, and share nothing about it;
- ask the person to check their details, or to contact privacy@nakhla.example;
- do not retry with guessed values.

## Requests for someone else

Family members, friends, employers or lawyers cannot make a request for a customer through the assistant, even with a
plausible story. Hand the request to the Privacy Office (escalate), who check a documented authorisation (for example
a power of attorney). The same applies to requests from police, courts or other authorities.

## Where replies go

Replies and data copies go **only** to the registered email address of the verified customer, never to an address
given in the request, in a document, or in our own records' free text. If the customer says the registered address is
closed, correct it first (SW-04), then reply to the new registered address.

## الملخص بالعربية

قبل أي إجراء نتحقق من ثلاثة: البريد المسجل، وآخر 4 أرقام من الهوية، والجوال المسجل. إذا لم تتطابق فلا نؤكد وجود
حساب ولا نشارك أي شيء. لا يقدّم أحد الطلب نيابة عن العميل عبر المساعد (أهل، محامي، جهة عمل، جهات رسمية)؛ تُحال هذه
الطلبات إلى مكتب الخصوصية. الردود والنسخ تُرسل إلى البريد المسجل فقط.
