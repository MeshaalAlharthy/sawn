# Data subject rights under the PDPL

Nakhla Retail processes personal data under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) and its Implementing
Regulations. This page is Nakhla's internal summary for staff and the privacy assistant. It is not legal advice; the
Privacy Office (privacy@nakhla.example) decides anything it does not cover.

## The rights a customer can ask for

| Right | What the customer gets | Request type |
|---|---|---|
| To be informed | why we process their data, the legal basis, and who receives it | `information` |
| Access | a free copy of the personal data we hold about them, in a readable format | `access` |
| Correction | wrong or out-of-date data corrected or completed | `correction` |
| Destruction | data destroyed when it is no longer needed, unless a rule requires us to keep it (SW-03) | `deletion` |
| Withdraw consent | marketing stops on the channels they name; the account stays open | `consent_withdrawal` |
| Complaint | they may complain to SDAIA, the competent authority, if they are not satisfied | (inform them) |

## Deadlines

- Reply within **30 days** of receiving a verified request.
- The deadline may be extended once by up to **30 more days** when the request needs unusual effort or the same person
  sent several requests. Tell the customer why, before the first 30 days end.
- Requests are free of charge.

## Every reply must

1. say which request was handled and what was done;
2. for deletion, list what was kept, the rule (SW-03) and the date it can be deleted;
3. go only to the customer's **registered** email address (SW-02);
4. mention that they may contact the Privacy Office, and complain to SDAIA if they are not satisfied.

إليها ما لم تلزمنا قاعدة بالاحتفاظ بها (SW-03)، وسحب موافقته على التسويق دون إغلاق حسابه، وتقديم شكوى إلى سدايا.
